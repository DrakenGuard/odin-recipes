Tinolang Manok Recipe

Description

A classic authentic filipino chicken soup

Ingredients

  • Chicken meat
  • 1-3 Garlic cloves
  • Pepper (white or black)
  • 150-250 Mg of Spinach
  • Water
  • A cube of chicken stock
  • Onion
  • 3-6 thumbs of ginger
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pot and saute ginger, onion, and garlic cloves.
  2. Add salt and pepper.
  3. When the onion gets soft, add the chicken and cook for around 5 minutes.
  4. Add water and the chicken cube and let it boil.
  5. Once the water is boiling, lower the heat and let it simmer for around 30-60 minutes.
  6. Add spinach into the pot and let it cook for 1-5 minutes.
  7. Serve in a serving dish with rice.
