Adobong Manok Recipe

Description

A classic and delicious filipino chicken recipe. It's sweet, tangy, and slightly sweet.

Ingredients

  • Chicken meat
  • Garlic cloves
  • Bay leaves
  • Soy sauce
  • Pepper
  • Vinegar
  • Brown sugar

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in a large mixing bowl for at least 30 minutes to 1 hour or overnight with soy sauce, vinegar, brown sugar, pepper, garlic, and bay leaves.
  2. In a large pot over medium-high heat, heat the cooking oil until hot then separate the chicken from its marinade while reserving the marinade and cook the chicken for about 2-3 minutes.
  3. Pour over the marinade over the large pot and bring to a boil.
  4. Once it's boiling, reduce the heat to a simmer and leave it for at least 30 minutes.
  5. If the chicken can be flipped, flip it and cook for another 15-30 minutes.
  6. Once you're done, you can garnish it with some green onions and serve with rice.
